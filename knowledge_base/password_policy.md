# Password & Account Policy

## Rules
- Minimum 12 characters.
- Must include upper, lower, number, and symbol.
- Passwords expire every 90 days.
- Last 5 passwords cannot be reused.
- Accounts lock after 5 failed attempts for 30 minutes.

## Self-service reset
1. Go to `https://reset.company.internal`
2. Enter corporate email.
3. Complete MFA verification.
4. Set a new password meeting policy rules.
5. Update password on laptop login, email, and VPN.

## Helpdesk-assisted reset
IT can trigger a temporary password only after identity verification:
- Employee ID + registered email match, OR
- Manager confirmation for urgent unlocks.

Temporary passwords expire in 24 hours and must be changed at first login.

## Account locked
- Wait 30 minutes, or request unlock via verified helpdesk flow.
- Do not keep retrying old passwords.

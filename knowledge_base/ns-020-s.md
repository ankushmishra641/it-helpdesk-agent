# Meeting screen sharing unavailable — service desk runbook
Category: Software
Article ID: NS-020-S
Owner: Northstar Software Operations
Audience: service-desk
Version: 1.0
Reviewed: 2026-10-20
Review due: 2027-01-20
Service: meeting

> Fictional Northstar Systems policy for this public demonstration. Not instructions for a real employer.

## Symptoms
The screen-share control is disabled or shows a permission error.

## Prerequisites
Use a managed Northstar demo device and the approved work account. Check for an active service incident before changing local settings. Public demo actions never affect real accounts.

## Resolution
1. Confirm the reported symptom: The screen-share control is disabled or shows a permission error.
2. Establish scope: affected user count, managed asset, location and time of onset. Check the meeting service snapshot.
3. Guide the employee through the approved steps: Check whether the meeting policy permits sharing. Grant only the required screen-recording permission to the approved app. Restart the app if the operating system requests it. Share a specific window to avoid exposing unrelated information.
4. Record which check changed the outcome. Do not mark resolved until the employee confirms the affected workflow works.
5. If unresolved: Route policy restrictions to Collaboration Operations with the meeting type and the exact error.

## Validation
Repeat the original affected workflow once. Record whether it succeeds, the service involved and the time. If it still fails, keep the ticket open and document the exact remaining symptom.

## Escalation
Route policy restrictions to Collaboration Operations with the meeting type and the exact error.
Use High priority for multi-user disruption or suspected compromise, Medium for one blocked employee, and Low for a routine request with a workaround.

## Evidence and privacy
Record the asset identifier, error code and redacted diagnostic summary. Never collect passwords, recovery keys, MFA codes, private documents or full message contents.

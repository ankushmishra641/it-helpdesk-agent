# Printer queue not available — service desk runbook
Category: Hardware
Article ID: NS-017-S
Owner: Northstar Hardware Operations
Audience: service-desk
Version: 1.0
Reviewed: 2026-10-20
Review due: 2027-01-20
Service: printing

> Fictional Northstar Systems policy for this public demonstration. Not instructions for a real employer.

## Symptoms
The office printer is missing or a print job remains queued.

## Prerequisites
Use a managed Northstar demo device and the approved work account. Check for an active service incident before changing local settings. Public demo actions never affect real accounts.

## Resolution
1. Confirm the reported symptom: The office printer is missing or a print job remains queued.
2. Establish scope: affected user count, managed asset, location and time of onset. Check the printing service snapshot.
3. Guide the employee through the approved steps: Join the secure office network or approved VPN. Select the printer for your office in Company Portal. Confirm the printer has no local paper or toner alert. Cancel only your own stuck jobs and retry a small non-sensitive page.
4. Record which check changed the outcome. Do not mark resolved until the employee confirms the affected workflow works.
5. If unresolved: Include the printer asset label, office and queue name. Do not attach confidential documents as examples.

## Validation
Repeat the original affected workflow once. Record whether it succeeds, the service involved and the time. If it still fails, keep the ticket open and document the exact remaining symptom.

## Escalation
Include the printer asset label, office and queue name. Do not attach confidential documents as examples.
Use High priority for multi-user disruption or suspected compromise, Medium for one blocked employee, and Low for a routine request with a workaround.

## Evidence and privacy
Record the asset identifier, error code and redacted diagnostic summary. Never collect passwords, recovery keys, MFA codes, private documents or full message contents.
